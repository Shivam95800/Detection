Kestrel Home Appliances - Operations Policy v4.1
Internal. Owner: D2C Operations.
§1 Scope
This policy covers warranty and Kestrel Shield claims submitted by authorised service centres, franchises and
freelance technicians (service partners).
§4 Costs used for decisions
A genuine claim held for review delays the customer's repair; Kestrel issues goodwill worth Rs 380 on average
when that happens.
A fraudulent claim paid out costs the full claim amount. Blended cost of a service contact: Rs 260.
§5 Claim approval
Claims are submitted by the service partner with the product serial, a fault description and, where available,
photos. Until 30 Apr 2026 every claim required partner inspection sign-off.
From 1 May 2026, claims under Rs 2,000 are auto-approved without inspection to cut turnaround time.
Larger claims still need inspection.
The claims investigation desk can review at most 40 claims per month. Investigation outcomes are recorded as
fraud / not fraud; cases still open at export are undecided.
§6 Partner onboarding
Kestrel onboarded about 60 new service partners over the past year to cover tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
§9 Systems and timestamps
Until 30 Sep 2025 service and order operations ran on the legacy Zoho Desk instance. From 1 Oct 2025
everything runs on the Kestrel CRM. Legacy records were migrated in bulk; the source column says which system
a row came from.
All timestamps in exports are Indian Standard Time as displayed in the CRM, except resolution events imported
from the legacy Zoho event log, which were stored in UTC and were not converted during migration.
§10 Data handling
Customer and operational data may be shared with approved vendors for analysis. It must not be published,
uploaded to public repositories or shared beyond the engagement team.
